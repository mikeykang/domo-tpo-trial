# Lane A1: syndicated / quantitative consumer data BY ETHNICITY (US, Canada)

Compiled 2026-09-09. Scope: published numbers on skincare product usage, purchase or spend by race/ethnicity, per product type, plus K-beauty buyer demographics. English-language sources only.

Status codes: READ = I read the number on the fetched page. SNIPPET = seen only in a search-result snippet (page blocked or paywalled). SECONDARY = read on a page that re-reports another publisher's number.

Method note: ~40 web searches and ~85 fetch attempts (about 30 returned 403/402/404/410 or paywalls). The session's shared search quota ran out at the end, so a few follow-ups were fetch-only.

## 2. Base rates

Population shares (US):
- Census Bureau, July 1 2023 (release 2024-06-27, READ): Hispanic 19.5% (65,219,145); non-Hispanic White 58% (195,432,584); non-Hispanic Asian 7.4% (20,685,425); non-Hispanic Black 42,313,088 (≈12.6%, share computed by the fetch summary, not printed). https://www.census.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2024/population-estimates-characteristics.html
- Brookings (Frey), 2024 estimates (2025-07-31, READ): White non-Hispanic 57.5%, Hispanic 20%, Black 12.6%, Asian American 6.7%, two-or-more races 2.5%. https://www.brookings.edu/articles/growing-diverse-and-immigrant-populations-drove-the-nations-post-pandemic-demographic-rebound-new-census-data-show/

Beauty buyer / dollar shares (NielsenIQ, READ):
- Hispanic: 14.4% of beauty households → 16.6% of beauty dollars (2024); 14.1% → 16.4% (2023); 17% → 20.3% (2022, different base). Over-index ratio ≈ 1.15x on dollars vs households.
- Black: 13.1% of BPC buyers → 12.8% of BPC dollars (2026); 12.5% of beauty dollars vs 14.4% of population (2024). Slight under-index on dollars overall; over-index in fragrance (15.9% HH → 22.4% $), hand & body lotion (14.8% → 16.6%).
- Asian American: spend per buyer +13% (2026), +18% (2024), +22.75% (2021) vs average beauty buyer; facial skincare buy-rate gaps in A1-08.

Sunscreen base rates (routine/consistent use, adults):
- NHW 40.4% / Hispanic 24.7% / NHB 10.9% (NCI 2022 via NASEM, READ); Asian 29% vs NHW 35% (NHANES 2011-2018, SNIPPET); Hispanic 44.6% vs NHW 54.5% for 2015-2018 (a different definition, SNIPPET); Black 11.4% for 2021-23 (SNIPPET).
- Daily sunscreen among young adults (UCL Open Env 2024, READ): MENA 36%, Asian 26%, Hispanic 24%, NHW 22%, NHB 15%.

Spend on personal care (BLS Consumer Expenditure Survey):
- 2023 narrative (SNIPPET, search result only): "In 2023, on average, Asian and Black consumers spent more than White and Hispanic consumers on personal care products." The 2024 race and Hispanic-origin tables exist at https://www.bls.gov/cex/tables/calendar-year/mean-item-share-average-standard-error/reference-person-race-2024.xlsx and .../reference-person-latino-2024.xlsx (link hrefs READ on https://www.bls.gov/cex/tables.htm) but BLS returned an HTML block page to the downloader, so the dollar values are NOT captured.

## 5. Ten strongest findings, ranked

1. Asian Americans out-buy the US average on every facial-skincare sub-category NielsenIQ lists: moisturizer +$53.51/yr, treatment +$17.22, cleanser +$16.70, eye +$15.08, toner +$5.47 (NIQ 2024-06-06, READ).
2. In Canada, K-beauty buyer indices are "significantly above the national average" for Chinese, Filipino and South Asian households; Quebec under-indexes (NIQ 2026-05-05, READ).
3. The US K-beauty boom is driven by "high-income, Asian American Millennials," with growth now spreading to broader segments (NIQ 2025-10-21, READ).
4. NielsenIQ names the Hispanic beauty consumer as over-indexing on the K-beauty trend, but publishes no index value (NIQ 2025-10-02, READ).
5. Hispanic and African American households make up 36% of TikTok Shop beauty spending, the channel where K-beauty grows fastest (Circana via WWD, Q4 2025, SNIPPET).
6. Sunscreen has the steepest ethnic gradient of any category: routine use NHW 40.4%, Hispanic 24.7%, NHB 10.9% (NCI 2022 via NASEM, READ); Asian 29% vs NHW 35% (SNIPPET).
7. Daily facial moisturizer: Asian 63% vs NHW 49%; daily facial cleanser: Asian 56%, Hispanic 55% vs NHW 41%; daily sunscreen: Asian 26%, Hispanic 24%, NHW 22%, NHB 15% (UCL Open Env 2024, n=770, READ).
8. The one direct toner datapoint runs against "toner → Hispanic": in a 2019 California sample Black women used toner most (2.2 days/wk) and Latinas least (0.9); Latinas led face sunscreen (4.0 days/wk) (CAPABLE 2023, n=318, READ).
9. Asian Americans are 17% more likely than average to use facial skincare, and toner, sheet masks and facial mists run "well above the norm" (Mintel 2023, READ + SECONDARY).
10. Hispanic households are 14.4% of beauty buyers but 16.6% of beauty dollars, with facial skin care named among the categories where they outspend non-Hispanics (NIQ 2024, 2023, READ). Black consumers are 13.1% of buyers but 12.8% of dollars, over-indexing in fragrance, bath and body rather than facial skincare (NIQ 2026, READ).
